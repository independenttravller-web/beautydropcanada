Drivers must follow delivery instructions, applicable law, safety requirements and BeautyDrop operating procedures. Driver classification, insurance requirements and contractor terms must be set out in a separate reviewed driver agreement.

Applicable fees, taxes, payment terms, cancellations and refunds are determined by the merchant agreement and BeautyDrop policies. Production payment processing must be configured before live paid service begins.
