Driver terms

BeautyDrop Driver / Contractor Agreement

Draft version 2026-09-03. Contractor classification, insurance and operating terms must be reviewed by Canadian legal counsel and an insurance professional before drivers perform paid work.

1. Eligibility

Drivers must hold any legally required licence, vehicle documentation, insurance and permissions applicable to the work they perform.

2. Safe operation

Drivers must comply with traffic law, safe-driving practices, product handling requirements and BeautyDrop delivery procedures.

3. Confidentiality and customer data

Customer delivery information may only be used to complete the assigned delivery and must not be retained, shared or used for unrelated purposes.

4. Pickup and delivery proof

Drivers must use required pickup codes, status updates, customer PINs and proof-of-delivery steps and must not falsify delivery records.

5. Compensation

The final signed agreement must state how drivers are paid, when payment occurs, permitted deductions and any expense responsibilities.

6. Independent contractor review

The parties should obtain legal advice on worker classification. Contract wording alone does not determine legal status.

7. Insurance and claims

Final insurance limits, collision reporting, cargo claims and incident procedures must be documented before launch.

8. Termination

BeautyDrop may suspend or terminate access for unsafe driving, fraud, misuse of customer data, failed verification, serious complaints or breach of operating requirements.
