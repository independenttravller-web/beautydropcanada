About BeautyDrop

Delivery infrastructure for local beauty retail.

BeautyDrop Canada was designed to help beauty stores offer reliable local delivery while keeping the store, driver and customer connected to the same order.

Why BeautyDrop exists

Beauty products are often urgent: hair appointments, events, replacements and everyday essentials do not always fit a trip across the city. BeautyDrop gives local retailers a delivery workflow built around that reality.

Our platform focuses on clear dispatching, verified pickup, customer updates, tracking and documented delivery completion.

For stores

Turn existing customer orders into local delivery requests.

For drivers

Use a structured workflow for pickup, transport and delivery proof.

For customers

Track the delivery and receive a more transparent handoff experience.
