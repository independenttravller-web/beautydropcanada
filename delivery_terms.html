Customer delivery terms

BeautyDrop Delivery Terms

Draft version 2026-09-03. These customer-facing delivery terms require final legal review before live use.

Delivery information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate address, contact and access information through the merchant handling their order.

Tracking and notifications

BeautyDrop may send transactional delivery updates relating to an active delivery. Message and data rates may apply depending on the customer’s mobile plan.

Delivery PIN

A delivery PIN may be used to verify handoff. Customers should provide it only when they are ready to receive the delivery and should not share it publicly.

Failed delivery

A delivery may be marked attempted or failed when the recipient cannot be reached, the address is inaccessible or completion would be unsafe. Re-delivery charges and return procedures are subject to the merchant’s final policy.

Proof of delivery

BeautyDrop may record delivery status, timestamp, notes and private proof images where appropriate. These records are used for operational support and claims handling.

Refunds and product issues

Product quality, order contents and retail refunds are generally handled by the selling merchant. Delivery-fee refund rules should be confirmed in the final commercial policy.
