Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026. This website copy is a production-ready starting draft and should be reviewed by Canadian legal counsel before commercial launch.

Information we collect

BeautyDrop may collect account information, store information, delivery recipient information, delivery addresses, phone numbers, order references, delivery status records, proof-of-delivery records, support communications, payment records and technical security logs.

How information is used

Information is used to operate deliveries, authenticate users, provide tracking and notifications, support customers and merchants, process payments, prevent fraud, maintain records and improve service reliability.

Delivery photos and proof

When enabled, delivery photos are stored in private object storage and linked to the applicable delivery. Access is limited to authorized operational users. BeautyDrop should maintain a documented retention and deletion schedule appropriate to its business and legal obligations.

SMS and communications

Phone numbers may be used for transactional delivery messages such as pickup, status, attempted delivery and completed-delivery notices. Marketing messages should only be sent where separate consent requirements are satisfied.

Service providers

BeautyDrop may use hosting, database, object storage, email, SMS, payment, monitoring and analytics service providers to operate the platform. Production contracts and privacy terms with those providers should be reviewed before launch.

Security

BeautyDrop uses access controls, secure password hashing, CSRF protection, rate limits, secure cookie settings and other safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Access and questions

Customers and business users may contact BeautyDrop using the contact page to ask questions about personal information or request appropriate access or correction.
