Merchant terms

BeautyDrop Merchant Agreement

Draft version 2026-09-03. This page is provided as an operational draft and must be reviewed by qualified Canadian legal counsel before commercial launch.

1. Merchant relationship

The merchant uses BeautyDrop to request local delivery services for lawful customer orders. The merchant remains responsible for the underlying retail sale and product compliance.

2. Accurate order information

The merchant must provide accurate recipient, address, phone, package, distance and delivery instruction information and must not knowingly submit fraudulent or prohibited deliveries.

3. Packaging and prohibited goods

The merchant is responsible for suitable packaging and for ensuring items are lawful and safe to transport. A final prohibited-goods schedule should be attached to the signed agreement.

4. Customer communications

The merchant must have a lawful basis to provide customer contact information to BeautyDrop and must provide required notice or consent for transactional delivery communications.

5. Fees, taxes and invoices

Delivery fees, payment timing, taxes, credits, cancellations and refunds are governed by the merchant’s final commercial rate schedule and invoice terms.

6. Delivery verification

BeautyDrop may use pickup codes, customer PINs, status events, notes, signatures and private delivery photos to document performance.

7. Liability and insurance

Final liability limits, cargo loss rules, claims procedures, insurance requirements and indemnities require legal and insurance review before use.

8. Suspension

BeautyDrop may suspend access for suspected fraud, unsafe activity, misuse, unpaid balances or material breach of platform rules.

9. Governing law

The final signed agreement should identify the applicable Canadian province, courts and dispute process after legal review.
